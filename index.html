About YMN Translation Services

In response to the need for top quality translations because of the growing Spanish speaking population in the country, YMN Translation Services was founded over twenty-two years ago.
Yolanda Morales Newcomb is a native Spanish speaker, born and raised in Puerto Rico. She holds a B.A. in Mathematics and Education from the University of Puerto Rico and earned a Master Degree in Business Management at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.
As a freelance translator, she has offered translation and interpreting services to a wide variety of businesses and non-profit organizations. Her vast experience includes translation of a very diverse array of documents in multiple fields. Her work and customer service are defined by excellence and dependability.
